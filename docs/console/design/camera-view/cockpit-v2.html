The Cockpit, second pass

First version's tapes read as scrollbars — no fill, no marked limits, and pinned to the bezel. These are the instrument ADR‑0009 actually specifies: a scale with a fill, the limits drawn on the scale, and a boxed reading at the bug. Annunciators and readouts now sit on a solid scrim so they hold against a bright sky.
