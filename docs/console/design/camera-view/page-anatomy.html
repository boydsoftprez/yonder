The deck also has room for the readout strip under the picture, which is where "what is this camera actually doing right now" belongs. The picture is smaller than in A; on a 1024 px tablet it is still about 750×420, which is more than enough to judge framing, focus and exposure.

The biggest possible picture, and the closest to a real display head. The cost is that a control panel covers the right of the frame while you use it — so on a camera you are aiming, the panel hides the part of the shot you are aiming at. It also needs a mode per panel, which multiplies what the build-time capture gate has to photograph (R‑UI‑12 already requires a page that hides part of itself to be captured in each of those parts).

Everything here is placeholder content in the real palette — the readouts, the commanded-vs-actual marks and the soft keys are drawn to scale so you can judge weight and density, not to fix any particular control. Click an option to select it, and tell me in the terminal what you actually think.
